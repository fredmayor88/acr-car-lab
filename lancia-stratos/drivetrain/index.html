How we worked it out

The reasoning and the in-game runs behind the numbers above.

Every speed on these pages is engine revs turned into road speed, from the gearing alone:

speed (km/h) = rpm × tyre circumference (m) × 0.06 ÷ total ratio

0.06 turns metres per minute into km/h: × 60 minutes per hour ÷ 1000 metres per kilometre.

total ratio = primary × gear × final drivetyre circumference = 2π × free radius × 0.9904

The free radius is the tyre's, read from the game files. The rolling factor, 0.9904, was fitted to the top speeds measured in game on 14 runs across seven cars (Stratos, 306 Maxi, Xsara WRC, 037, 206 WRC, Delta Integrale and Impreza).

The rev limit, 8450 rpm, was measured in game with telemetry. The end of the torque curve in the game files is not the limiter: on every car here the curve runs past it.

The Stratos has a Primary Gear setting, and its gear sets also store a primary of their own, 33//31*31//30 (1.100). The runs distinguish between:

  1. The Primary Gear you pick replaces the gear set's primary: primary = Primary Gear.
  2. The Primary Gear you pick multiplies it: primary = Primary Gear × 33//31*31//30.

All three gear sets were run on Primary Gear 35//30*33//28 with Differential Ratio Rear 65//17. On gear set 3 the predictions for 5th were about 198 km/h if it replaces and 180 km/h if it multiplies. The speedometer read 197, and gear sets 1 and 2 agreed, supporting primary = Primary Gear.

So the primary is the Primary Gear you pick, and the final drive is Differential Ratio Rear alone, the one ratio below the gearbox in the game files:

primary = Primary Gearfinal drive = Differential Ratio Rear
