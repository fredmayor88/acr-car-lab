How we worked it out

The reasoning and the in-game runs behind the numbers above.

Every speed on these pages is worked out from engine revs and the gearing alone:

speed (km/h) = rpm × tyre circumference (m) × 0.06 ÷ total ratio

rpm × tyre circumference ÷ total ratio is metres per minute; × 0.06 (× 60 minutes per hour ÷ 1000 metres per kilometre) turns that into km/h.

total ratio = primary × gear × final drivetyre circumference = 2π × free radius × 0.9780

The free radius is the tyre radius stored in the game files. The rolling factor, 0.9780, allows for a loaded tyre rolling on a slightly smaller radius; it was fitted to the top speeds measured in game on 15 runs across eight cars (Stratos, 306 Maxi, Xsara WRC, 037, 206 WRC, Delta Integrale, Impreza and GTA Junior).

The rev limit, 7020 rpm, was measured in game with telemetry. The end of the torque curve in the game files is not the limiter: on most cars here the curve runs past it.

The Quattro has no centre differential. Its front path has Differential Ratio Front and its rear path Differential Ratio Rear:

front path ratio = Differential Ratio Frontrear path ratio = Differential Ratio Rear

The final drive formula is taken from the Delta Integrale, 206 WRC and Impreza, where it was measured:

final drive = (front path ratio + rear path ratio) ÷ 2final drive = (Differential Ratio Front + Differential Ratio Rear) ÷ 2

It cannot be tested here. A test needs different front and rear ratios, and with no centre differential they make the axles fight and the car hard to control. With both set the same, as the car ships, the final drive is simply that ratio.
