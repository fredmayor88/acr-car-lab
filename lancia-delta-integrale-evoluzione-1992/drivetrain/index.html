How we worked it out

The reasoning and the in-game runs behind the numbers above.

Every speed on these pages is worked out from engine revs and the gearing alone:

speed (km/h) = rpm × tyre circumference (m) × 0.06 ÷ total ratio

rpm × tyre circumference ÷ total ratio is metres per minute; × 0.06 (× 60 minutes per hour ÷ 1000 metres per kilometre) turns that into km/h.

total ratio = primary × gear × final drivetyre circumference = 2π × free radius × 0.9904

The free radius is the tyre radius stored in the game files. The rolling factor, 0.9904, allows for a loaded tyre rolling on a slightly smaller radius; it was fitted to the top speeds measured in game on 14 runs across seven cars (Stratos, 306 Maxi, Xsara WRC, 037, 206 WRC, Delta Integrale and Impreza).

The rev limit, 7200 rpm, was measured in game with telemetry. The end of the torque curve in the game files is not the limiter: on every car here the curve runs past it.

On the Delta the game files put Center Differential Ratio before the centre differential. The front drivetrain path has no additional selectable reduction; the rear path adds Center Ratio to Rear and Differential Ratio Rear:

front path ratio = Center Differential Ratiorear path ratio = Center Differential Ratio × Center Ratio to Rear × Differential Ratio Rear

With all four wheels turning at the same road speed, the centre differential's input turns at (front output speed + rear output speed) ÷ 2, so the equivalent final drive is:

final drive = (front path ratio + rear path ratio) ÷ 2final drive = Center Differential Ratio × (1 + Center Ratio to Rear × Differential Ratio Rear) ÷ 2

The first run could not distinguish between two possibilities:

  1. The selected Center Ratio to Rear and Differential Ratio Rear are ignored and their stock values stay in effect: final drive = Center Differential Ratio × 13//34 × 30//12.
  2. Final drive = (front path ratio + rear path ratio) ÷ 2, with the selected rear ratios.

It was run on Center Differential Ratio 55//12 with the rear ratios close to stock: Center Ratio to Rear 13//34 and Differential Ratio Rear 34//14. With those settings the two possibilities put 6th at about 182 and 181 km/h, only about 1% apart, too little to distinguish reliably with the in-game speedometer. The speedometer read 180.

For the second run Center Differential Ratio stayed on 55//12 and the rear ratios were deliberately moved far from stock: Center Ratio to Rear 17//38 and Differential Ratio Rear 34//13. This produced clearly different predictions for 6th: about 182 km/h under possibility 1, or 161 km/h under possibility 2. The measured speed was 160 km/h, supporting final drive = (front path ratio + rear path ratio) ÷ 2.
