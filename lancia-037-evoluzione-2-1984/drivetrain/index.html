How we worked it out

The reasoning and the in-game runs behind the numbers above.

Every speed on these pages is revs turned into road speed: rpm × tyre circumference ÷ total ratio, where the total ratio is primary × gear × final drive. The circumference comes from the tyre's free radius in the game files, times a rolling factor of 0.9904, fitted to the top speeds measured in game on 14 runs across seven cars (Stratos, 306 Maxi, Xsara WRC, 037, 206 WRC, Delta Integrale and Impreza).

The rev limit, 8450 rpm, was measured in game with telemetry. The end of the torque curve in the game files is not the limiter: on every car here the curve runs past it.

The final drive is Differential Ratio Rear alone, the one ratio below the gearbox in the game files. The run on gear set 2 with Differential Ratio Rear 46//9 is one of the runs the rolling factor was fitted on, and it fits that reading.
