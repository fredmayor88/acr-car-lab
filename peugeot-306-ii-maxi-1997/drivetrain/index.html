How we worked it out

The reasoning and the in-game runs behind the numbers above.

Every speed on these pages is worked out from engine revs and the gearing alone:

speed (km/h) = rpm × tyre circumference (m) × 0.06 ÷ total ratio

rpm × tyre circumference ÷ total ratio is metres per minute; × 0.06 (× 60 minutes per hour ÷ 1000 metres per kilometre) turns that into km/h.

total ratio = primary × gear × final drivetyre circumference = 2π × free radius × 0.9780

The free radius is the tyre radius stored in the game files. The rolling factor, 0.9780, allows for a loaded tyre rolling on a slightly smaller radius; it was fitted to the top speeds measured in game on 15 runs across eight cars (Stratos, 306 Maxi, Xsara WRC, 037, 206 WRC, Delta Integrale, Impreza and GTA Junior).

The rev limit, 9600 rpm, was measured in game with telemetry. The end of the torque curve in the game files is not the limiter: on most cars here the curve runs past it.

The final drive is Differential Ratio Front alone, the one ratio below the gearbox in the game files:

final drive = Differential Ratio Front

A run on gear set 4 with Differential Ratio Front 68//12 fits that formula: the speedometer read 183 km/h in 6th, where it puts 6th at 185. That run is also one of those the rolling factor was fitted on.
