How we worked it out

The reasoning and the in-game runs behind the numbers above.

Every speed on these pages is revs turned into road speed: rpm × tyre circumference ÷ total ratio, where the total ratio is primary × gear × final drive. The circumference comes from the tyre's free radius in the game files, times a rolling factor of 0.9904, fitted to the top speeds Fred measured on 14 runs across seven cars (Stratos, 306 Maxi, Xsara WRC, 037, 206 WRC, Delta Integrale and Impreza).

The rev limit, 7400 rpm, was measured in game with telemetry. The end of the torque curve in the game files is not the limiter: on every car here the curve runs past it.

On the Xsara, Center Differential Ratio is the only ratio setup offers. The front and rear differentials are fixed in the game files, 2.778 at the front and 2.786 at the rear, so the final drive is Center Differential Ratio × their average.

Fred's one run, on gear set 1 with Center Differential Ratio 39//24, fits that: 6th read 183 km/h, where the formula puts it at 182. The two fixed differentials are only 0.3% apart, so a run can't tell their average from either one alone; the average follows the rule measured on the Delta Integrale, 206 WRC and Impreza.

Why an average: between the gearbox and the axles the drivetrain splits the gearbox output to the front and the rear. With all four wheels at the same road speed, the gearbox turns at the average of what the two axles ask of it.
